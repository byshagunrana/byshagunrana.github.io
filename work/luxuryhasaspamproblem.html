Executive brief

The client does not experience departments. She experiences one Maison.

A client advisor may have a new arrival worth showing. CRM has a campaign to activate. Events has seats to fill. Client service has an update. Beauty has a launch. Each touch may make sense on its own. The client receives the sum.

BCG and Altagamma report that top tier luxury clients can engage with as many as 57 brands and receive roughly 40 to 60 instances of brand outreach a month. Sixty five percent reported feeling overwhelmed by excessive communication and insufficient personalization.1

Current Dior client engagement roles publicly span client tiers, retail teams, events, CRM, client intelligence and both one to one and one to many activations.234

Strategic thesis: the objective should not be raw contact volume. It should be relationship value per interruption.

03 · The Luxury Contact Cadence Framework

Every outbound opportunity has to earn the interruption.

The framework evaluates each contact in the context of the relationship that already exists. A campaign calendar can create an opportunity. It cannot, by itself, create permission to interrupt.

Human contactTaste, memory or discretion matter.

A person should make the contact because the context depends on relationship knowledge.

Maison contactUseful information deserves delivery.

The message is relevant but does not need to manufacture personal intimacy.

Ambient presenceRemain discoverable without demanding attention.

Editorial, social, windows and self directed browsing can keep the Maison culturally present.

Strategic silenceThe interruption costs more than the next message adds.

No direct contact is a valid recommendation rather than a missed commercial action.

Positive signalsCosts

Relevance · client signal · relationship value · scarcity · timing sensitivity · service need

Interruption cost · saturation

A contact should not become more justified merely because the client is valuable. The more valuable the relationship, the more expensive irrelevance becomes.

06 · Client journeys

Four clients, four different definitions of good service.

C101The Collector

Established taste, privacy sensitive, active across high consideration categories.

Prioritize advisor led relevance and protect quiet periods after appointments and major purchases.
C204The Emerging Patron

Rapidly engaged and broadening category interest.

Allow more tempo when it follows real signals. Use contact to deepen taste knowledge, not merely frequency.
C318The Occasion Client

High spend but episodic behaviour tied to gifting and significant moments.

Time communication around occasions and service needs rather than assuming constant shopping intent.
C427The Dormant Loyalist

Historically valuable, currently quiet and weakly responsive.

Use one considered intervention, then wait. Dormancy is information.

07 · Measurement system

Replace activity metrics with relationship health metrics.

Contact Load

Direct Maison contacts received by one client in a rolling 30 day window.

Relevant Contact Ratio

Share of direct contact tied to a strong client signal, need or relationship context.

Contact Collision Rate

Share of direct contacts arriving inside a defined window after another Maison touch.

Ignored Touch Streak

Consecutive direct contacts without meaningful client response.

Client Initiated Ratio

Share of meaningful interactions initiated by the client.

Quiet Period Compliance

Share of protected quiet periods respected by all contact owners.

Advisor Signal Share

Personal outreach grounded in advisor observation rather than generic campaign instruction.

Relationship Progression

Movement into deeper category, appointment, service or experience engagement over time.

08 · Operating model

One client, one contact ledger.

Shared contact ledger

Advisor, CRM, events, service and beauty contacts appear in one chronological view where legally and operationally appropriate.

Priority hierarchy

Client requests and service responsibilities outrank commercial calendars. Personal relevance outranks generic urgency.

Protected quiet periods

Selected appointments, purchases or explicit client signals can defer lower value commercial activity.

Named relationship owner

For high value relationships, one advisor or client lead has visibility over important planned contact without becoming a bottleneck for service.

Reason coded overrides

Managers can override suppression, but they record why. Exceptions become learning.

Weekly pressure review

CRM and retail review unusual contact load, repeated ignored touches and multiple planned invitations.

09 · Validation research

The next step is not another model. It is listening to real clients.

Perceived load

How many direct messages, calls, emails or invitations did the client receive from the luxury brand that contacts them most often?

Perceived relevance

How many felt connected to something the client had bought, asked about or shown interest in?

Fatigue behaviour

Has the client ever ignored a luxury brand because it contacted them too often?

Quiet period preference

After a private appointment or major purchase, how soon would the client want contact again when there is no service issue?

Advisor ownership

Would the client prefer one named advisor to coordinate important contact when other teams are involved?

Meaning of silence

If a brand deliberately contacted the client less, what would make that feel elegant rather than neglectful?

No survey responses are fabricated in this project. The instrument exists to validate the framework with real clients before any claim about behaviour is made.

10 · Implementation

Pilot before platform.

The proposal should begin with controlled learning, not a major technology programme.

Weeks 1 to 4Contact audit

Map every direct touch source for two pilot boutiques and establish baseline contact load, collisions and ignored touch measures.

Can the Maison see total client pressure accurately enough to govern it?
Weeks 5 to 8Shadow scoring

Run recommendations without suppression. Compare system decisions with advisor judgment and record disagreement.

Do the variables capture what experienced advisors consider relevant?
Weeks 9 to 16Controlled pilot

Apply suppression and ambient routing to a selected cohort while protecting service and client initiated exceptions.

Does contact quality improve while commercial performance remains credible?
Weeks 17 to 20Calibration

Adjust weights, thresholds, quiet periods and client state logic using observed outcomes and qualitative feedback.

Is the model stable enough to scale?
Week 21 onwardScale decision

Extend only if relationship metrics improve without material deterioration in retention, appointments or client value.

Should the cadence layer become part of standard governance?

Recommendation

Strategic silence is not the absence of clienteling. It is a clienteling decision made in defense of future attention.

The answer is not romantic minimalism. It is orchestration. A Maison should know which contact deserves a human being, which information deserves direct delivery, which story can remain ambient, and which moment should simply be left alone.

The target is not a quieter CRM system. It is a more intelligent relationship.
