The Recognition Floor.

The Lady Dior after Jonathan Anderson.

How do you modernise an icon without breaking the memory that makes it an icon? I wanted to look at that question beyond personal taste: what Dior has kept, what it is changing, how shoppers are reacting, and what a brand team might need to govern.

01 · Observed evidence

Anderson is changing the story around the bag more than its underlying grammar.

Observations are drawn from public evidence, not confidential Dior design data. A high score in the audit is a score under an original proposed framework, not proof that a real consumer would recognise a bag.

The core stays remarkably stable.

Across twelve current Lady Dior product descriptions, the most visible changes cluster in colour, embroidery, botanical motifs and charm treatments. Architectural shape, top handles, proportion and the familiar charm mechanism are strikingly persistent.

Dior still tells the 1995 Lady Diana story. The Lady Art editions also show how much experimentation the bag can absorb when its silhouette remains readable.

New interest is not universal approval.

Of eight public 2026 discussion threads coded in the accompanying workbook, seven carry a positive overall directional code and one a negative one. Some describe Anderson details as a reason for first-time interest; others object to bows, sweetness or perceived value.

Those discussions are self-selected anecdotes, not a representative survey or evidence of conversion lift.

02 · Code archaeology

Some codes carry recognition. Others have room to play.

Code layerExamplesJob
Recognition anchorsStructure, Cannage, top handles, D.I.O.R. charms, proportion, craft continuityKeep the object mentally identifiable through designer changes
Elastic codesColour, material, embroidery, surface design, hardware finishProvide novelty without rewriting the whole bag
Narrative codesClovers, ladybirds, bows, flowers, seasonal stories, Lady ArtCreate current meaning and give change a story
Treating every code as sacred freezes an icon. Treating every code as flexible dissolves it.

Cannage receives a high proposed weight because it remains recognisable across materials and colourways. Dior links the current clover and ladybird details to Christian Dior's documented superstitions: House lineage gives newer treatments a reason beyond trend.

03 · Original proposal

Recognition needs a floor, not a freeze.

Model outputs describe this sample and weighting scheme—not measured Dior brand equity.

I designed an Icon Evolution Framework based on six recognisable anchors. The provisional 75/100 floor is a management review trigger—not an experimentally discovered threshold.

What the current audit returned.

The twelve-product sample averages 98.3/100 for recognition and 48.4/100 for novelty under the analyst model. Nine products occupy the Evolution or Collector lanes. None falls below the proposed floor.

Where recognition debt begins.

In a hypothetical stress test, removing Cannage and materially changing the silhouette together produces a score of 60. Removing Cannage alone reaches 75. These are formula outputs, not respondent results.

Why a score is not a conclusionWeights and thresholds are analyst choices; only a controlled study can measure whether people still identify an altered object as Lady Dior.
04 · Validation and limitations

The first proper test is with people, not just a spreadsheet.

The next stage would use blinded bag stimuli, unaided identification and recognition confidence, comparing people with different levels of Dior familiarity. Each change should be isolated before testing cumulative departures. Desirability should also be measured: an object can remain recognisable while becoming less desirable, or the reverse.

The project proposes a Code Change Note for internal review: which anchors remain, which changed, why the changes belong to the Maison, and how the team would evaluate the response after launch.

Research integrityThere is no commissioned Dior consumer panel, no access to internal commercial data and no validated consumer recognition probability. This is independently developed desk research, a twelve-product audit, eight public discussions and a proposed governance system.
