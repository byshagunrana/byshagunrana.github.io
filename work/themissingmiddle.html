The Missing Middle.

Finding tomorrow's VIC before spend makes them obvious.

A client can be growing closer to a Maison long before annual spend makes that relationship commercially visible. I wanted to understand what a luxury team might notice earlier—and how to do it without guessing someone's wealth or turning service into surveillance.

01 · The question

A spend ladder tells you what happened. It doesn't always tell you what is forming.

Two clients with identical historical spend can be moving in opposite directions. An advisor needs to distinguish acceleration from disengagement without making social-status assumptions.

The question became unusually concrete.

Christian Dior Couture's 2026 Client Engagement Director listing explicitly includes developing high-potential mid-tier clients, monthly outreach planning, client mapping and planned business. That confirms client development is an actual luxury retail responsibility, not an invented commercial problem.

But what is a 'high-potential' client?

BCG and Altagamma reported in 2025 that 70% of potential top-tier clients are missed when segmentation or CRM definitions depend on restrictive or static approaches. The goal should not be to build a more intrusive wealth model. It should be to understand an existing relationship earlier.

Trajectory before tier. Evidence before privilege. Human judgment before treatment.

02 · The public evidence

Real shoppers say things a transaction-only model may miss.

Public posts are useful for understanding kinds of behaviour that exist. They are not evidence of what Dior's actual client population looks like.

To avoid pretending I had a client portfolio, I coded twelve anonymised public 2026 shopper observations—only the behaviours actually stated by the posters. A zero in the dataset means a positive signal was not evidenced in that comment, not that the person's behaviour was absent.

Signals in the 12 cases

Six cases include direct advisor, appointment or event evidence; seven include stated future intent; eight contain collector or rarity signals. These are sample counts, not population estimates.

What the demonstration score says

The mean score across the twelve examples is 37.3/100. Five cases score at least 45 and enter the proposed advisor-review queue. A higher score is not a probability of becoming a VIC.

03 · The proposed framework

Four dimensions. Twelve interpretable signals. No automatic VIP decisions.

The proposed Future VIC Development Model distinguishes commercial activity from Maison depth, the quality of the human relationship and explicit intention.

DimensionWeightEvidence it would examine
Economic Momentum30Purchase velocity, acceleration and exceptional-product interest
Maison Depth25Category breadth, progression and collector behaviour
Relationship Engagement25Advisor continuity, appointments, events and preference knowledge
Intent Signals20Explicit future intentions, planned business, sourcing and ownership continuity
Step 01

Identify

Use consented first-party transaction and relationship information, not postcodes, appearance, surnames or inferred wealth.

Step 02

Review

A transparent score creates an advisor task. A human validates the hypothesis and can challenge or reject it.

Step 03

Act carefully

Offer the least intrusive relevant treatment—sometimes information or sourcing, sometimes a conversation, sometimes silence.

The model is intentionally not an automated tier upgrade. It makes a candidate review queue for a client advisor; it does not decide whose status changes or what anyone deserves.

Evidence boundaryThe dimension weights are original governance choices, not trained coefficients or Dior's internal CRM logic. No historical Dior transaction database was available, so the model has not demonstrated predictive accuracy.
04 · What would make this safe enough to test

The model should deepen relevance, not increase contact pressure.

Deployment requires retrospective proof first, a tightly governed advisor experiment second and a decision only after commercial and relationship measures both pass.

Non-negotiable design controls

Exclude sensitive personal characteristics and inferred wealth proxies. Display each score's actual inputs. Treat declining an event or not purchasing as context—not evidence of lower worth. Keep the advisor's judgment and the client's preferences in the loop.

A real evaluation

Freeze a historical date; score only information available at that point; compare with a spend-and-RFM baseline over the following twelve months. Test identification lift together with client satisfaction, contact pressure, fairness and false-positive patterns.

The proposed management dashboard tracks advisor-validated opportunity rate, progression across categories, retained relationships, contact pressure and top-tier progression against a simple baseline. A commercial increase that damages the relationship would not count as success.
