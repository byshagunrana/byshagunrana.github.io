How I work

I separate evidence, interpretation and what needs testing.

That distinction keeps the work ambitious without confusing a strong hypothesis with a verified fact.

01

Observe

Public evidence, customer journeys, brand codes, communication, product and culture.

02

Frame

Translate the pattern into a strategic tension, define the question worth solving, and surface what still needs validation.

03

Build

Turn the tension into an operating idea with a clear rationale, implementation logic and a way to test whether it works.
