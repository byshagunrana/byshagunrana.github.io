My Weltanschauung · 2020 to now

I refuse to clean up six years of becoming.

The old writing is not here to prove I was always right. It is here to prove I was always willing to think, make things and change my mind in public.

Before I called it strategy

I was already trying to make people care.

At fifteen and sixteen, that meant writing online, social impact projects, Girl Up, menstrual health work, fundraising and trying to make ideas understandable enough that somebody would actually act on them.

I did not have the language for positioning, audience, narrative or behaviour yet. I just knew that the way an idea was presented changed whether people paid attention.

Different subject. Same question: how do you make something matter to someone else?

2020
to
now

The original WordPress site remains online as a time capsule. Some of it makes me proud. Some of it makes me want to edit the version of me who wrote it. Both belong there.

Older work should be read in the age and moment it came from. I would write some of it differently now. That is exactly why I am keeping it.

Open My Weltanschauung
2020

I started writing during lockdown. Books, films, culture, social issues and whatever else refused to leave my head.

2021

Writing became doing. Community work, campaigns, menstrual health projects, fundraising and learning that ideas need people around them.

Then

Interests changed. My voice changed. Some years were louder than others. The point was never perfect consistency.

Now

Luxury, brand strategy, consumer psychology and entrepreneurship have become the questions I want to study with much more discipline.

What the archive proves to me

I did not discover curiosity for an application. I have receipts.

The subject matter evolved. The habit did not: notice something, ask why, make something, put it in the world, learn, repeat.
