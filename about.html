About

I have always been more interested in the question behind the question.

This is the part where a normal portfolio would give you a polished paragraph. I would rather show you how the pieces actually connect.

A real photograph will live here. Not an artificial one.

I started with words.

I began writing online as a teenager during lockdown. The subjects changed constantly because I did too. Books, films, culture, social questions, projects, opinions. The archive is messy, but it is honest. It taught me that I like finding patterns in how people think and what they care about.

Then I studied systems.

I studied computer science. I am grateful for the way it trained me to break complicated things into parts, look for structure and ask what happens when one decision changes the whole system. I also realized that the problems I wanted to spend my life on were increasingly human rather than technical.

Luxury made the threads meet.

Branding brought together many things I had been circling for years: psychology, culture, aesthetics, storytelling, commerce and human behaviour. Luxury made those relationships especially visible because every detail has to carry meaning.

I am building toward ownership.

I want to become exceptionally good at brand strategy and marketing, learn from people who operate at the highest level, and eventually create companies of my own. I am interested in work that can be commercially strong without becoming emotionally empty.
