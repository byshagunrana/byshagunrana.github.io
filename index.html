This website has layers

I did not wake up one morning and decide to become a luxury strategist.

2020

I started writing.

During lockdown, this site was simply where I put whatever I could not stop thinking about. Literature, films, culture, social issues, random observations and occasionally something completely unrelated.

THEN

I kept following questions.

Writing made me notice people. Technology made me notice systems. Projects and social work made me care about what those systems actually do to people’s lives.

NOW

I study desire.

Today I am fascinated by luxury, branding, consumer behaviour, culture, creativity and entrepreneurship, especially the details that turn products into symbols and customers into believers.

I took the scenic route

I studied computer science and became much more interested in why people buy lipstick than in writing software.

Technology taught me to think in systems. Writing taught me to notice people. Luxury introduced me to a world where psychology, storytelling, craft, culture and business collide.

I am now building toward a career in luxury brand strategy and marketing, with the long term goal of creating companies of my own.
