The route here was not tidy

Computer science taught me systems. Writing taught me people. Brand strategy gave me somewhere to put both.

I studied computer science, but the questions that kept pulling me back were human ones: why people care, what they notice, what they remember, and what makes an idea worth acting on.

Now I am building toward luxury brand strategy and marketing. Long term, I want to create companies of my own. I want to understand the craft before I own the outcome.
