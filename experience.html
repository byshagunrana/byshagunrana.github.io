Nykaa Fashion

Brand Management Intern, Luxe Division

Worked with designer representatives and category managers on onboarding, catalogues and listing readiness. My direct coordination covered 15+ labels within a broader onboarding effort involving 20+ designer brands. I reviewed product details and imagery, flagged inconsistencies and supported designer edits, festive campaigns and promotional content.

What stayed with me was how easily a designer's distinctiveness could disappear online. If the fabric, construction or handwork wasn't explained, the customer saw little more than a photograph and a price. I began asking how you make a collection easy to find without making it feel interchangeable with everything else on the platform.
